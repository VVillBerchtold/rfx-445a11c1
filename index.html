Staff Referral Directory (Internal)

This site lists active provider offices, what insurance plans each accepts, and basic contact/booking details, for use by staff answering referral questions. It is generated from the ReferralFinderX master specialist list.

How to read insurance status: each office page lists insurance plans ACCEPTED and NOT accepted by name. If a plan is not mentioned either way for an office, its acceptance is unconfirmed - verify by phone.

Geography: Rhode Island offices are grouped by county (Providence County, Kent County, Washington County, Newport County, Bristol County). "Providence" the city is inside Providence County. Some offices are in Massachusetts or Connecticut.
